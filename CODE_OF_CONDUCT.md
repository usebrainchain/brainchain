# Code of Conduct

BrainChain follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion focused on the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated in any project space, including issues, pull requests and discussions.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **hello@usebrainchain.com**. Reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to enforce this code.
